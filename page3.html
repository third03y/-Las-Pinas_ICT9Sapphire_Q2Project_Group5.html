Welcome to O.B. Montessori Center!

Thank you for considering our school for your child’s education. Our application process is now online for your convenience.

We look forward to receiving your application and welcoming you to the O.B. Montessori Center community!

How to apply

Please follow these steps to complete your application:

  1. 1 Fill out the online Application Form: Complete all sections of the form accurately.
  2. 2 Review the list of requirements: View the list of documents needed for submission.
  3. 3 Upload required documents: Scan and upload the necessary documents. Once uploaded, click the SUBMIT button.
  4. 4 Confirmation receipt: You will receive an SMS confirmation with your application ID and password for tracking purposes.
  5. 5 Await evaluation: The Admissions Office will review your application and inform you of the evaluation outcome.
